## 1. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, and have no access to:

- Your name, email address, phone number, or other contact details
- Account credentials (we do not require account creation or login)
- Usage analytics, behavioral data, or app interaction logs
- Device identifiers, advertising IDs, or IP addresses
- Location data
- Photos, contacts, or other personal files, unless you explicitly choose to import them into the App for its core functionality (see Section 2)
- Payment or financial information (if the App is paid, this is handled entirely by the app store — see Section 5)
- Crash reports or diagnostic data sent to us

We do not operate servers that receive, log, or store any of the above. There is no backend, database, or cloud account associated with the App that has access to your personal information.

## 2. Information Stored on Your Device

The App may create, use, or store certain data **locally on your device only**, in order to function. Depending on the App, this may include:

- App settings and preferences
- Content you create or input within the App (e.g., notes, entries, files)
- Local cache or temporary files needed for the App to run

This data:

- Is stored exclusively in the App's local storage or your device's file system
- Is never transmitted to us, or to any third party, by the App
- Remains under your control — you can delete it at any time by clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App
- Is not backed up by us in any way (if your device includes its own OS-level backup, such as iCloud or Google backup, that is controlled by your device settings, not by us — see Section 4)

## 3. No Data Sharing or Third-Party Access

Because we do not collect any data, there is nothing for us to share, sell, rent, or disclose to third parties, advertisers, or data brokers. We do not have a database of users, and there is no data that could be subject to a data breach on our end, because no user data resides on our servers.

Since your data stays on your device, its security is primarily governed by your device's own security features (passcode, encryption, biometric lock, etc.). We recommend keeping your device's operating system updated and using a secure lock screen to protect locally stored App data.

Because we do not collect or store any personal data on our servers, there is no personal data held by us for these rights to apply to. Any data associated with your use of the App exists solely on your device and is already fully within your control — you can view, export, or delete it at any time directly through the App or your device settings.
