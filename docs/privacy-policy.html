Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 2026-07-29
Last Updated: 2026-07-29

Introduction

Gurmukhi Sikho ("the App," "we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our approach to your information when you use Gurmukhi Sikho (the "App").

The short version: we do not collect, store, transmit, or have access to any of your personal data or information. Everything the App creates or uses stays on your device.

This policy is written to be clear and specific about what that means in practice.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, and have no access to:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or other contact details
  • Account credentials (we do not require account creation or login)
  • Usage analytics, behavioral data, or app interaction logs
  • Device identifiers, advertising IDs, or IP addresses
  • Location data
  • Photos, contacts, or other personal files (see Section 2)
  • Payment or financial information

We do not operate servers that receive, log, or store any of the above. There is no backend, database, or cloud account associated with the App that has access to your personal information.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

The App may create, use, or store certain data locally on your device only, in order to function. This may include:

  • App settings and preferences
  • Content you create or input within the App (e.g., lesson progress information, game progress information)
  • Lessons and chapters data
  • Games data
  • Local cache or temporary files needed for the App to run

This data:

  • Is stored exclusively in the App's local storage or your device's file system
  • Is never transmitted to us, or to any third party, by the App
  • Remains under your control — you can delete it at any time by clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App
  • Is not backed up by us in any way (if your device includes its own OS-level backup, such as iCloud or Google backup, that is controlled by your device settings, not by us — see Section 4)

3. No Data Sharing or Third-Party Access

Because we do not collect any data, there is nothing for us to share, sell, rent, or disclose to third parties, advertisers, or data brokers. We do not have a database of users, and there is no data that could be subject to a data breach on our end, because no user data resides on our servers.

4. Third-Party Services and Device Features

The App does not integrate third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking tools.

If the App uses any of the following, those services operate independently of us and are governed by their own privacy policies, not this one:

  • App store platforms (Apple App Store / Google Play): Handle downloads, and installation. Their own privacy practices apply to that process.
  • Operating system features (e.g., device backups, notifications): Controlled by your device's own settings, not by the App.

We do not embed any third-party SDK that transmits data off your device. If this changes in the future, we will update this policy and, where required, seek your consent.

5. Permissions

The App may request the following device permissions solely to provide its core functionality:

  • [e.g., Internal Storage] — used to [purpose], processed locally, never transmitted

Granting a permission does not result in that data leaving your device. You can revoke any permission at any time through your device settings, which may affect certain App features.

6. Children's Privacy

Because the App does not collect any personal information from any user, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the relevant minimum age in your jurisdiction). If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us, though as noted, the App is not designed to receive or store such information in the first place.

7. Data Security

Since your data stays on your device, its security is primarily governed by your device's own security features (passcode, encryption, biometric lock, etc.). We recommend keeping your device's operating system updated and using a secure lock screen to protect locally stored App data.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights under laws such as the GDPR (EU/UK), CCPA/CPRA (California), or similar regulations, including rights to access, delete, or port your data. Because we do not collect or store any personal data on our servers, there is no personal data held by us for these rights to apply to. Any data associated with your use of the App exists solely on your device and is already fully within your control — you can view, export, or delete it at any time directly through the App or your device settings.

9. International Users

The App does not transmit data across borders, because it does not transmit data at all. Your locally stored data remains on your device regardless of your location.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example to reflect changes in the App's functionality. We will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy. Since the App does not collect contact information, we may not be able to notify you directly — we encourage you to review this policy periodically.
